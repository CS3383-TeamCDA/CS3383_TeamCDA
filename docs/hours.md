# Hours Tracking for Team 0 (CDA)

## Updating Your Tasks

Each task should be updated by the team member who completes it.

**Only update the yellow fields.**

Enter your:

- Hours worked
- Current task status
- Meeting attendance
- Evidence or notes, when applicable

All totals, costs, completion percentages, and related metrics update automatically. Do not manually change formula-driven cells.
